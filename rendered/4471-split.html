#4471 · what the merge held: two problems, not one

owner support desk living reviewed 2026-10-31 · 0 days ago

#4471 says one issue and one fix. The 412 reports merged into it are not one issue. Read against the tenant directory (locale and booking form), they split into two causes. Only the first is the parser bug the ticket describes.

The parser fix will not stop the second problem. It has no ticket of its own until the tickets below are filed.

The two problems

#problemreportstenantsseen
1The shared parser reads every date month first, so day-first tenants get flipped dates379112026-04-01 → 2026-10-04
2Booking form v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field, so people in day-first tenants type month first3332026-04-17 → 2026-09-22

379 + 33 = 412. Every report is counted once, where the desk files it. The export holds 427 rows because its pages overlap; 15 reports appear twice and are counted once.

Footnote to problem 1. 5 of its 379 reports read as a one-day move, not a month flip (R-41202, R-41209, R-41236, R-41344, R-41620; the desk lists them in its own notes). They are filed under the parser and counted there. Their bodies talk about time zones and day boundaries (the same meeting giving two cities two different days), so ask engineering to check whether the parser fix covers them once it ships.

How to tell them apart

  • Look up the tenant, not the report. Every tenant in the directory writes day first. Whether the cause is the parser or the form hint depends on which booking form the tenant runs and how the report arrived. Only a report that came through a booking-v3 form is problem 2. The parser flips every other day-first report.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess. It is made from the reporter’s browser. 23 reports it tagged as hint are the parser bug, and 15 it tagged as parser are the form hint.

Why the merge hid this

Decision 0001 merges reports on subject line alone. The subjects here are things like “Wrong day on the invite”, which fit both problems. Merging on subject is fast, but it counts two bugs once and hides one of them. The reports were merged into #4471 months before anyone read the bodies; the oldest merge in the export is dated 2026-07-02.

What to do

  1. File one ticket per problem (the tickets for this page are in the repository’s issues).
  2. Move the problem 2 reports out of #4471, so the parser fix closes only what it fixes.
  3. Reopen the question in decision 0001: when two reports share a subject, check the tenant’s locale and booking form before merging. A new record replaces 0001; 0001 is not edited.

Lessons

  • A big ticket is mostly one bug reported many times, until it isn’t. Count the causes before you merge, not after.
  • A number that can be computed should be computed. These counts come from analysis/count.mjs, which reads the export and prints them; re-run it when the export changes.
